The following source datasets are included for **scholarly research and educational
purposes**. They remain copyright of their respective rights holders and are
**not** covered by the MIT licence. Users are responsible for complying with the
terms of the original sources where applicable.

### Baby Calendar (bc) — 2008–2022

- **Rights holder:** © 株式会社ベビーカレンダー (Baby Calendar Co., Ltd.)
- **Source:** <https://baby-calendar.jp/>
- **Included as:** Raw Excel workbook (`data/jmena 2008-2022.xlsx`) and derived TSV exports.
- **Reuse:** Research and educational use with full attribution. Not for commercial redistribution.

### Meiji Yasuda Life Insurance (meiji) — 1912–2025

- **Rights holder:** © 明治安田生命保険相互会社 (Meiji Yasuda Life Insurance Company)
- **Source:** <https://www.meijiyasuda.co.jp/enjoy/ranking/>
- **Included as:** Downloaded JSON files, combined CSV, derived TSV exports, and archived
  press release PDFs (`data/meiji_yasuda_data/press_releases/`).
- **Reuse:** Research and educational use with full attribution. Not for commercial redistribution.

### Heisei Namae Jiten (hs) — 1989–2009

- **Rights holder:** © 平成名前辞典 (Heisei Name Dictionary)
- **Source:** <https://www.namaejiten.com/>
- **Included as:** Raw plain-text files (`data/heisei/`) and derived TSV exports.
- **Reuse:** Research and educational use with full attribution. Not for commercial redistribution.

### Japanese Birth Data (BD)

- **Rights holder:** © 国立社会保障・人口問題研究所 (National Institute of Population and Social Security Research, IPSS)
